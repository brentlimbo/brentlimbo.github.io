01 Behind the closet

Personal style.
Shared with someone new.

I’m inspired by the way streetwear, vintage pieces, and everyday clothing can come together to express someone’s individual style. Through Brent’s Closet, I curate clothes with that mix in mind, giving pre-owned pieces a second life instead of leaving them unworn.

My selection is geared toward teens and young adults who enjoy streetwear, vintage, and casual wear. From statement denim to easy-to-style jackets, I look for pieces that fit that audience while reflecting my own taste.

With over 20 completed online sales, I’ve handled pricing and customer communication myself. Running the shop lets me put branding and marketing into practice—connecting the clothes I choose with the people who want to wear them.
